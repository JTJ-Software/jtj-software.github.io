Working principles

A method that supports the work.

The process adapts to the nature of the work, while these principles keep decisions and progress clear.

  1. 01

    Context before tools

    Start with goals, constraints, users and operations before choosing a technical direction.

  2. 02

    Visible decisions

    Make trade-offs explicit so that progress stays understandable and choices can be revisited.

  3. 03

    Useful progress

    Move through concrete outcomes that reduce uncertainty and create something worth evaluating.

  4. 04

    Maintainable outcomes

    Build with the people, operations and future changes around the work in mind.

Engineering judgment

The right tools, without dogma.

Technology is selected for fit rather than fashion. The best choice is the one that serves the context, remains clear to maintain and gives the work room to evolve.
